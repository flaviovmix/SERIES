Cinematic photorealistic film still, the Nile near Giza around 2560 BC: two wooden cargo boats with a single mast and steering oars, decks loaded with cut white limestone blocks, being poled toward a stone quay where workers wait with ropes and wooden sledges; on the plateau in the distance the pale unfinished pyramid; a man on the quay holds a papyrus roll and counts the blocks. Golden late afternoon light, reflections on the water, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, muted natural color grading, no text, no captions, no watermark, no modern objects.
